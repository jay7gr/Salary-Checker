§ 02 — METHOD

How we get to the number.

Three layers of adjustment, applied in order. You can read the long-form methodology, or trust that we sweat the details so you don't have to.

i.
Cost of living
An index built from Numbeo, EIU, Mercer, and government CPI data, normalised to NYC = 100.
Numbeo · OECD · ECB
ii.
Local tax
Effective income tax and social-security rates for each country, applied to your specific gross.
Updated quarterly
iii.
Real expenses
Rent, groceries, transport, healthcare, childcare — by neighbourhood where data exists, by city otherwise.
