— The method

Three layers, one honest number.

i. — Cost of living

A real index, not vibes.

Numbeo, EIU, Mercer, and government CPI data, normalised so New York City equals 100. Updated quarterly. No ad-hoc adjustments.

ii. — Tax & deductions

Effective rates, not headlines.

Country-level income tax brackets and social-security contributions, applied to your specific gross. Effective, not marginal — what you'll actually keep.

iii. — Real expenses

Down to the neighbourhood.

Rent, groceries, transport, healthcare, childcare. By neighbourhood where the data exists, by city otherwise. Always the latest source pull.
