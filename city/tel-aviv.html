About Tel Aviv

Tel Aviv has a cost of living index of 81.3, making it a high cost city compared to New York (baseline = 100). It ranks #10 out of 113 cities tracked globally.

The local currency is ILS. A salary of $75,000 USD in New York would need to be approximately ₪219,473 in Tel Aviv to maintain the same purchasing power.

The approximate effective income tax rate in Israel is 16% for a mid-range earner. Including social security and other mandatory deductions, the total deduction rate is approximately 28.5%.
