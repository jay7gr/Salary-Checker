I. Our Philosophy

Longevity is not a treatment.
It is a way of living.

At Kloster Lebensquell we do not believe in quick cures. We believe in the slow, patient work of restoring a human being to themselves — body, mind, and spirit — with the quiet rigour the monks of this house have practised for nearly nine hundred years.

Each guest receives a single, bespoke programme of integrative medicine, psychosomatic therapy, therapeutic movement, and contemplative rest. We accept only a handful of guests at a time. Absolute discretion is the condition of our work.

— Dr. med. Friederike von Auenheim
Medical Director

III. The Monastery

Nine centuries of stillness,
ten kilometres from the Alps.

Founded in 1143 by Benedictine monks, our monastery has been a place of retreat for nearly nine hundred years. After a decade of restoration — guided by the Bavarian Landesamt für Denkmalpflege — its cells, chapel, refectory, and orchards have been quietly transformed into a contemporary clinic without erasing a single beam of spruce.

The estate extends across forty hectares of forest, meadow, and spring-fed lakes. There are fourteen guest cells, a single long-table refectory, a restored baroque library, and a thermal bathhouse built over a natural spring the monks have used since the thirteenth century.

IV.

A day at Kloster Lebensquell

Rhythm is the first medicine. Each day is measured by the monastery's bronze bells — seven rings, seven invitations to breathe.

06:30

Lauds

Silent walk through the cloister and orchards. Cold plunge for those so inclined.

07:30

Diagnostics

Morning laboratory, HRV, continuous glucose readings reviewed with your physician.

09:00

Therapeutic work

Infusion, hyperbaric, or physiotherapy, according to the day's protocol.

12:00

Refectory

A single seasonal meal prepared by our kitchen from the monastery gardens. Silence is optional.

14:30

Psychotherapy

One-to-one session with your assigned psychotherapist or psychiatrist.

16:00

Movement

Alpine hike, Pilates, swim in the lake, or stillness in the bathhouse. Your choice.

19:00

Vespers

Sound bath in the baroque library, followed by a light supper or intermittent fast.
