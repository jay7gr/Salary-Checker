The Programmes

A single, bespoke programme
for each guest.

No two stays at Kloster Lebensquell resemble one another. After an initial two-day diagnostic admission, your lead physician composes a programme drawn from four disciplines — measured to the day, revised weekly.

01 Medical Detoxification

Renewing the cellular terrain.

Our detoxification protocols are grounded in a careful diagnostic panel — heavy-metal burden, mitochondrial function, gut microbiome, inflammatory markers — and calibrated individually. We do not prescribe off-the-shelf cures.

  • Physician-supervised modified fasting (Buchinger method)
  • Intravenous chelation and glutathione therapy
  • Ozone autohaemotherapy and hyperbaric oxygen
  • Liver and lymphatic drainage
  • Colon hydrotherapy, when clinically indicated
  • Daily laboratory-guided adjustment
02 Therapeutic Movement

Movement as medicine.

Our head of movement, Lukas Feldmann, is a former Olympic physiotherapist and a certified mountain guide. Every session is private and built on objective measurement — VO₂max, gait analysis, force plates — rather than on trend.

  • Private alpine hiking with a resident guide
  • Functional strength and mobility
  • Classical Pilates and Gyrotonic
  • Qi Gong and breath-led yoga
  • Forest bathing (Shinrin-yoku) in our forty-hectare woodland
  • Cold exposure and lake swimming (seasonal)
03 Contemplative Relaxation

A liturgy of rest.

Rest is the least understood medicine of our age. At Kloster Lebensquell we have rebuilt a complete contemplative infrastructure — silent hours, bathhouse, sound, and liturgy — so that our guests may relearn how to stop.

  • Roman-Irish baths in the restored monastery cellar
  • Kneipp hydrotherapy over natural springs
  • Infrared saunas and cryotherapy
  • Sound and singing-bowl therapy in the baroque library
  • Guided silence and contemplative reading (lectio divina)
  • HRV-guided biofeedback and sleep optimisation
04 Psychosomatic Care

The conversation of a life.

Under the direction of Prof. Dr. Matthias Kranzberg, our psychosomatic department is the quiet heart of the clinic. We treat burnout, addiction, grief, trauma, and the subtler disturbances of an accomplished life — with the discretion the subject demands.

  • Daily one-to-one psychotherapy or psychoanalysis
  • Psychiatric consultation and, where indicated, medication review
  • Trauma-specific protocols (EMDR, somatic experiencing)
  • Addiction medicine and relapse-prevention planning
  • Couple and family sessions on request
  • Art, music, and equine-assisted therapy

Begin with a confidential conversation.

A programme cannot be bought from a menu. Every admission begins with a private consultation, by phone or video, with a member of the medical team.
