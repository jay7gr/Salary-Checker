II. Arrival

How to find us.

Kloster Lebensquell sits in the Mangfall valley of Upper Bavaria, fifty-five minutes by road from Munich International Airport. We are pleased to arrange private transfer, helicopter reception at Munich Heliport, or rail collection at Rosenheim for arriving guests.

  • From Munich Airport (MUC)55 minutes by car
  • From Salzburg (SZG)1 hour 10 minutes
  • Nearest railwayRosenheim — 18 minutes
  • Nearest heliportMunich-Riem
